1 HMTA and C6H6O3 (phloroglucinol) were added to a 15 mL 2-neck 
flask w/ a condenser and schlenk adapter and evacuated to 100 mtorr 
and backfilled w/ N2 (g) 3x. Then transfered to another fume hood 
nitrogen line w/ a bubbler. 
2 conc. TFA was added and the rxn was heated to 100 oC at reflux 
(12:30 pm). 
- rxn turned yellow within 45 minutes, however not all SM was 
dissolved 
- rxn also was not at a reflux temp at 100 oC. 
- As rxn progressed it got more yellow  
3 HCl was added at 3:30 pm and rxn was still bright yellow. 
- rxn stayed bright yellow the entire time and solids were in there the 
entire time 
4 At 5:00 pm the rxn was cooled to RT and quenched w/ water 
5 solution (neon yellow) was filtered through celite but some browm 
stuff came through and I think the celite plug was dirty,  
* I also think the heat was not working during the rxn and that's likely 
why it stayed yellow and insoluble the whole time.
