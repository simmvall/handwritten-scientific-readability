1 Phloroglucinol and HMTA were added to a RBF w/ a condenser and 
TFA was added. 
- This time I'm trying it without making solids air free. 
- TFA fumed a lot when added to the RBF so it was added slowly. 
- color changed from bright yellow to bright orange within 5 min. 
2 Rxn was heated to 100 C and stirred (12:00 pm). 
3 3M HCl was added at 2:30 PM and rxn turned into a deep red which 
overtime turned into a deep orange. 
4 At 3:30 pm rxn was taken off heat and cooled to RT. 
5 orange solution was filtered w/ celite and water and DCM and then 
extracted w/ DCM 3x, the organic layers were then extracted w/ water 2x 
and brine 1x and rotovapped down 
6 An NMR in CDCl3 was taken. 
* most of the orange color stays in the aqueous phase. The product is a 
very very pale yellow 
0.08 g product  40% yield.
