Reaction Started at 12:30 
1. Reagent A was added to a RBF w/ a stirbar 
2. most of the DMF was added so that there was a wet solution. Raney 
Ni was weighed directly into the flask. (b/c the raney Ni is in a slurry it is 
difficult to measure and ~3g was added, a lot more than needed) 
3. The remaining DMF was added and used to wash any Raney Ni off 
the walls. 
4. N2H4 was added dropwise and gently swirled while added. Extra 
caution was taken to make sure the solution did not bubble over. A 
condenser was added (w/no top to make sure there was no pressure 
build up) 
5. The rxn was heated to 155 oC and refluxed and stirred vigorously. 
6. After ~4 hrs the rxn was filtered while hot through celite and washed 
w/ hot DMF (extra caution was taken to make sure raney Ni didn’t dry 
out and it was immediently covered w/ sand once the wash was 
complete.   
7. The clear filtrate was collected and the product was crashed out w/ 
water and then ice. (too much DMF was used during the filtration step 
so a very large amount of water had to be used. This could have 
potentially impacted the yield.)
