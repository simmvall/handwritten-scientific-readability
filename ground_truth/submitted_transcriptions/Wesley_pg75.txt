- SnCl2 was placed in a clean dry 10 mL RBF
- HCl was added to the solution and heated to 120 °C w/ a condensor
- 1 was made into a slurry w/ 2.25 mL of EtOH
- slurry added slowly to Boiling solution
- Solution Refluxed for 45 min then left to stir @ RT overnight
- HCl and EtOH were then removed via distillation
- Resedue was taken up in 2.25 mL of EtOH
- 50% w/w NaOH was added to Flask and mixed for 15 min
- Solution quenched w/ H2O and extracted w/ CHCl3
- CHCl3 was dried over Na2SO4 and removed in vac.
- Loss of some product due to bump during distillation.
- Yield = 43.5 mg
  → 54.4%
- HNMR = Good a lil DCM present
- Oil @ Room temp w/ a deep Red color
