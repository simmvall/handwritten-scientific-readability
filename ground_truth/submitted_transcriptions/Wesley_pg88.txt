- CsF & a stir bar were placed in a clean dry 50 mL Schlenk flask
- Under vac. the system was flam dried
- Remaining solids were added & system was evacuated & refilled x3 w/ N2
- Dioxane & MeOH were added
- System was freeze pump thawed 1 time
- solution heated to 85 °C
- Rxn Quenched @ 25h mark w/ H2O
- Extracted x4 w/ EtOAc, Organics washed w/ Brine, Dried over Na2SO4, Ran through Silica plug, & had solvent removed under vac.
- Crude was recrystalized using EtOH (~60 mL)
- Recrystal product washed w/ cold EtOH & cold Hexanes.
- Yield = 429.5 mg
  → 62.5% Yield
