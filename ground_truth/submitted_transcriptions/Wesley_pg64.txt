- Ice bath was set up
- NaH was added to a Clean Dry 250 mL Schlenk flask w/ a stir bar and 15 mL of THF. (Under N2 gas)
- 1 was mixed w/ 37 mL of THF (anhydrous)
- Temperature was kept ≈ 0 °C for reaction vessel
- 1 in THF was added dropwise to NaH/THF solution in ICE bath (Deep Red)
- Upon completion solution was mixed for 5 min in ICE
- 17 mL of THF & 9.71 mL of MeI were mixed and slowly added to the reaction vessel.
- Reaction mixed under N2 in Ice bath w/o refilling Ice
- Rxn Quenched 22h w/ 50 mL Brine 50 mL H2O
- Extracted w/ EtOAc
- Ran a 30 g wide column 2% EtOAc & separated Top 2 dots from rest
- NMR showed column was 74% Product & 26% -Br, try 2% THF column
- Sublimation test 40 °C High Vac. → 42 mTorr for 30 min led to → Pure product unsublimed & mixture of both as sublimed, 42% P & 58% -Br in sublimed product, try ~~lower~~ w/ High P & 45 °C
- Recrystal EtOH → 89% Pure Product by mass
  3.8984
  ↳ 39.3% Yield
