- NaH was added to a clean Dry 25 mL RBF that was filled w/ N2 gas
- The system was lowered into a dry ice/Acetone bath & 2 mL of THF were added
- A solution of 1 & 12 mL of THF was prepared and slowly added to the RBF, the remaining 2 mL was used to wash out solution container into NaH solution
- Solution mixed for 5 minutes then MeI was added dropwise to the alkyline solution (solution turns Red as reaction proceeds
  Solution removed from bath after addition & mixed @ RT 24 h
  Rxn quenched w/ H2O & Brine, (Product slightly H2O soluble)
  Extracted w/ EtOAc & CHCl3 (≈ 250 mL total) due to low solubility, Dried over Na2SO4, solvent Removed under red. pressure.
