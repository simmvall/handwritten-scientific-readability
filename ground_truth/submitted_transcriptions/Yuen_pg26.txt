PROJECT: Making COF-102

3/27/2014
YAL-I-25

Part (I): Making sealed pyrex tube

(1) Take a long glass tube, then use a glass "ruler" to determine the markings where the glass is going to get cut
(2) Cut the glass into 2 pieces using a glass cutter & snap/pop them open
(3) Use propane-flame to break open/seal one end of the tube
← tip of the sealed tube
* try to make sure the bottom is more or less rounded.
(4) Let the tubes cool to RT.

Part (II): Making COF.
Start: 7pm

Prepare 4 samples of COF-102

COF-102 YAL-I-25a → from YAL-I-16 (TBA batch #) YAL-I-25b → from YAL-I-20 YAL-I-25c → from YAL-I-22 YAL-I-25d → from YAL-I-23 85°C 120°C

(1) Weigh out ~50 mg of tetraboronic acid (TBA), and put them inside the tube.
(2) Sonicate for a few seconds to let the solid gravitate toward the bottom
(3) Add 0.5 mL of mesitylene and 0.5 mL of anhydrous dioxane to each tube.
(4) Flash frozen the tube @ 77K, i.e. immerse the tube into liquid N2 for a minute or two.
